## Selection criteria

Sources are evaluated for freshness, activity, popularity, depth and community health. The roster is rebalanced automatically; manual status locks can preserve an explicit curator decision. Scores and dates describe the last stored observations, not a live availability check.

## Nominate a source

Use the [source nomination form](https://github.com/JuanTorchia/awesome-curated/issues/new?template=nominate-source.yml) and explain its scope, recent maintenance, review process and overlap with the sources above. A nomination or a merged tool submission is not automatic admission to the roster. Two listings do not by themselves establish two independent tracked sources.

Tools are discovered through these sources; they are not added directly on request. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md) for the intake and publication rules.
